\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Sends out events relating to a market gathering. 

Acts as a concrete subject in the observer design pattern. Sends out events relating to a market gathering so that different components of the market can handle them and update in their own ways. \begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
