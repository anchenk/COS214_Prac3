\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Composite participant in the Composite pattern. 

Represents a container that can hold multiple \doxylink{classEventComponent}{Event\+Component} children (both leaves and other composites). Implements recursive operations by delegating to all children.

Also acts as a Concrete \doxylink{classSubject}{Subject} in the \doxylink{classObserver}{Observer} pattern, allowing children to register as observers and receive cascading notifications.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Implements the Composite role in the Composite pattern. 

Implements the Concrete \doxylink{classSubject}{Subject} role in the \doxylink{classObserver}{Observer} pattern. 

Does NOT implement \doxylink{classObserver}{Observer} directly -\/ receives via \doxylink{classEventComponent}{Event\+Component} inheritance.
\end{DoxyNote}
