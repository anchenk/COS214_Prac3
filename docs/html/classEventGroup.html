Detailed Description

Composite participant in the Composite pattern.

Represents a container that can hold multiple EventComponent children (both leaves and other composites). Implements recursive operations by delegating to all children.

Also acts as a Concrete Subject in the Observer pattern, allowing children to register as observers and receive cascading notifications.

Note
Implements the Composite role in the Composite pattern.
Implements the Concrete Subject role in the Observer pattern.
Does NOT implement Observer directly - receives via EventComponent inheritance.
